Website Accessibility Statement

HubSpot is committed to digital inclusion principles by striving to meet or exceed the requirements for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA Conformance (WCAG 2.1 Level AA) for its public-facing websites. We are constantly working towards improving the accessibility of our public-facing websites to provide an inclusive experience to all users of our website, including but not limited to those with visual, hearing, cognitive and motor impairments.

If you encounter accessibility barriers on our public-facing websites let us know by emailing website-accessibility@hubspot.com. Please be sure to specify the webpage and assistive technology (if applicable), and we will make reasonable efforts to meet your needs.
