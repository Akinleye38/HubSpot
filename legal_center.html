Legal Center

The Legal Center is your comprehensive resource for HubSpot's terms, policies, and agreements that guide your relationship with us. We've organized everything based on who you are and what you're doing—whether you're a customer, partner, or just exploring our site. Our goal is simple: make legal information clear, accessible, and easy to understand.
